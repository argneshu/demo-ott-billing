# Personas

## Standard Subscriber ("Sam") - primary
- Pays $20/month for the Standard plan on StreamPlex (Full HD, 2 screens, 2 downloads); signs in with email and password.
- Goal: get Premium features (4K, 4 screens, 6 downloads, Dolby Vision) now, without contacting support, paying only for the rest of the cycle.
- Frustration: the Billing page is read-only, so any plan change takes an out-of-band process.
- Stories: 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.4, 1.5

## Premium Subscriber ("Priya") - secondary
- Already on the $40/month Premium plan.
- Goal: see her plan and perks accurately, and never be offered an upgrade she cannot take.
- Stories: 1.2 (rejection), 1.3 (no button)
